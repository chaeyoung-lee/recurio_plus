Simulation assumptions & data provenance

This is a direct-to-gateway traffic visualization. All camera and gateway positions are fictional. BLE circles illustrate reach only; no BLE communication, shared-memory matching or adaptive routing occurs.

Historical mode counts every unique image file matched to a valid timestamp in the JRBP spreadsheet, grouped by calendar month and camera. All cameras share the same clock; empty months are retained, and observations are never looped. Colors use a fixed log(1 + count) scale so both light activity and large bursts remain visible. Monthly frames preserve long-term traffic trends, not the timing of individual captures within a month. A zero cell means no recorded images, not confirmed camera downtime. Timestamps retain their recorded local values; timezone and clock synchronization have not been established.

Historical energy is accounting across the entire recorded workload, without battery depletion suppressing sightings. Battery maintenance is unknown, so lifetime battery percentages are not inferred. Each image is modeled as 6,000 bytes + 40 provenance bytes + 32 header bytes; LoRa rate 5.5 kbit/s, transmit power 120 mW, capture/processing 6 mJ. Seeded fixed link success probabilities scale expected airtime by 1/p. Counts are real; radio parameters and payload sizes are assumptions. Gateway energy, idle draw, fragmentation, contention, queues and duty-cycle restrictions are not modeled.

Historical cost bars show the current month relative to the highest monthly cost in this dataset. Synthetic mode retains a one-minute step and a 12-step rolling average relative to its first step. Cumulative costs increase. The historical export contains monthly camera totals and one representative observation per occupied cell (the latest timestamp), not an individual-image ledger. Original filenames and timestamps are retained for those samples; thumbnails remain local.
